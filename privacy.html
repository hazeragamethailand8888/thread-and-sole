Privacy Policy - Thread & Sole 1

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Summary

Thread & Sole 1 ("the game") does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (save slots) and your settings (language, volume) are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network

The game files are delivered from our web host (GitHub Pages). Like any web server, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request). We do not receive or use this information to identify you. After the first load the game can run offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
